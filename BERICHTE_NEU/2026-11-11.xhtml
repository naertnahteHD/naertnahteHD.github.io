Councilmember Phan West concedes 1st council district race

(LEFT) Amy Phan West, Republican-unendorsed, official portrait 2023
(RIGHT) David Johnson, Democratic-endorsed, campaign portrait 2026

Amy Phan West conceded the race for Westminster's 1st council district to her Democratic-endorsed challenger David Johnson in what appears to be an AI-generated post. She will have served four years on the Westminster City Council. During the 2022 midterm elections, Phan West defeated her opponent John Gentile—Westminster Parks and Recreation commissioner who sought to "bring real change" and "stop the bullshit"—by only 21 votes.

@amyphanwest (Instagram) on 10th November 2026
"While the election results were not what we had hoped for, I wholeheartedly respect the decision of the voters."

Just two days prior to the post, Phan West, who did not receive an endorsement from the Republican Party, had hinted at the possibility of conceding. She was "preparing [herself] for the possibility that [she] may not return for another term", admitting that this election "has been a difficult cycle for Republicans across California and the nation".

@amyphanwest (Instagram) on 8th November 2026
"Lower Republican turnout and shifting voter support have contributed to results we must reflect on, learn from, and rebuild."

Republicans aren't likely to hold Westminster's mayoral seat, either. Republican Margie L. Rice was first elected mayor in 2000, serving until 2012 after which Republican Tri D. Ta succeeded her. He was mayor for ten years before he was elected to the represent the California's 72nd assembly district in 2022. Republican Chi Charlie M. Nguyen replaced Ta, and has served a total of four years so far.

Nguyen, who also did not receive an endorsement from the Republican Party, is running for re-election. He initially held a slight lead over his Democratic-endorsed challenger, Carlos Manzo. Over the weekend, the gap closed and Manzo overtook Nguyen. The Republican voter base was split between Nguyen and Kimberly Ho, a retired councilmember.

The only Republican leading in Westminster is NamQuan Nguyen. He was elected in 2022 with Phan West and is running for re-election. Despite this, his lead over his Democratic-endorsed challenger Loan Nguyen has been shrinking (N. Nguyen had a little more than 40% of the votes last week). The seat will flip if L. Nguyen wins the seat.


Amy Phan West's council record

Phan West—and councilmember Nguyen—first drew controversy after she attempted to fire city manager Christine Cordon. The move to fire Cordon stemmed from a tense exchange regarding trust with city staff and the integrity of the RFP process behind city-owned billboards on 24th May 2023. The firing attempt faced strong public backlash and ultimately failed on 12th July 2023, with Ho, Manzo, and mayor Nguyen—henceforth referred to as the council majority—voting against it in a closed session.

Tension in the council chambers was tangible and dysfunction became rampant with the election of councilmembers Nguyen and Phan West to city council. On 12th June 2024, Phan West was given three months by the city council majority to cease the unruly behaviour. Councilmembers spent hours arguing about the contents of a memo that explained why Phan West was facing a censure.

Staff report/memorandum: "Request [by Ho and Manzo] for a Resolution of Censure, Based on Actions of Councilwoman Amy Phan West (District 1)."

Regardless, the city council majority ultimately decided to direct city staff to prepare a resolution of censure against Phan West the following week, after she made a Facebook post denouncing them. They had determined that she had no desire to change or improve. Phan West accused the city council majority of silencing District 1. On 14th August 2024, the resolution was adopted, with councilmembers Nguyen and Phan West dissenting.

Resolution: "A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF WESTMINSTER, CALIFORNIA, CENSURING COUNCILWOMAN AMY PHAN WEST (DISTRICT 1) FOR UNACCEPTABLE CONDUCT"

On top of the censure, a lawsuit was brought against councilmembers Nguyen and Phan West during the 23rd October 2024 closed session meeting. Both were not present. The city council majority cited the lack of behavioral change from the two, whose disruptions hadn't yet ceased. The suit was eventually settled.

Lawsuit filing: 30-2024-01443472-CU-MC-CJC "City of Westminster v. AMY PHAN WEST, NAMQUAN NGUYEN"

The Orange County Register article cited by the filing (Exhibit A) states that N. Nguyen and Phan West "have been skipping key meethings this summer, filibustering city business".

Phan West faced trouble outside City Hall, as well. In January 2025, the Orange County District Attorney (O.C.D.A.) charged her with one misdemeanor count of bribery after she allegedly told parking control officers, who were impounding her husband's abandoned Jeep, that she was in the process of getting them raises. She pleaded not guilty and the O.C.D.A. agreed to allow her to participate in a judicial diversion program.

Press release: "Westminster City Councilmember Charged with Trying to Bribe City Parking Control Officer to Avoid Having Husband's Jeep Towed"

Videoclip:

The O.C.D.A. pushed for an extension of Phan West's diversion on the grounds that she had violated the terms of the diversion. Judge Sherri L. Honer, who oversaw the hearing regarding this issue in August 2026, had determined that Phan West did not violate the diversion terms. Judge Honer ruled that the diversion was completed and the charges be dropped from Phan West's record.

Near the end of the 15th October 2025 special council meeting, Phan West moved to direct the Westminster Police Department to cooperate with Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE). She claimed that cooperation with ICE would protect residents and businesses. The motion died on the floor.

At the same meeting, Phan West moved to have the city council consider declaring 14th October annually as "Charlie Kirk Day". The motion, which was met with severe public controversy yet again, was seconded by councilmember Nguyen and supported by mayor Nguyen. Councilmembers Manzo and Mark Nguyen—who replaced Ho in 2024—dissented.

Resolution: "A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF WESTMINSTER, CALIFORNIA, DECLARING EACH OCTOBER 14 AS CHARLIE KIRK DAY IN THE CITY OF WESTMINSTER"

Staff report: "Redesignation of Street Name Signs in Honor of Charlie Kirk on All American Way between Westminster Boulevard and 13th Street"

On 12th November 2025, Manzo was the only member of the city council to oppose both Charlie Kirk Day and Charlie Kirk Way. Members of the public turned out in numbers to oppose both agenda items. M. Nguyen had voted for Charlie Kirk Day despite initially opposing it last month.

On 10th December 2025, Phan West moved to designate yet another street; this time, to Sylvia Mendez on Main Street at Goldenwest Street. Public speakers stated that they greatly appreciated Sylvia Mendez and her legacy, but couldn't understand why Phan West would want to waste more taxpayer money on another street sign. The motion died on the floor.

Staff report: "Consider Request to Change Street Name Sign at the Intersection of Goldenwest Street and Main Street (at the entrance to Westminster High School) in Honor of Sylvia Mendez"

On 25th February 2026, councilmembers Manzo, M. Nguyen, and N. Nguyen accused Phan West of violating Government Code § 8314 during the 2026 Tet Parade. After much back-and-forth, Manzo declared her incapable of apologising for her wrongdoings. Thus, M. Nguyen motioned to ban her from speaking at future City public events for the remainder of her term. Phan West dissented, whilst N. Nguyen abstained from the vote.

Despite the ban, Phan West marched on stage at the city's "Vietnam War Veterans' Day" recognition ceremony on 29th March 2026 and began speaking. Her microphone was cut off.

On 26th August 2026—after Judge Honer determined Phan West did not violate her diversion terms—the speaking ban was reversed, with Manzo being the lone dissenter.

Amy Phan West's electoral history

Earlier this year, she ran for California's 45th congressional district to unseat incumbent Democrat Derek Tran, who won more than half of the votes. She came last place under four other Republicans, including mayor Nguyen.

During the 2024 Presidential General Election, she ran for director of Midway City Sanitary District and came 3rd. To win, she was required to come 1st or 2nd. Phan West successfully ran for Orange County Republican Party Central Committee in the primary election.

Prior to running for city council in 2022, she came 3rd in the race for California's 47th congressional district. Democrat Katie Porter won that race. Phan West won more votes than Republican Brian Burley of Huntington Beach, but came under Republican Scott Baugh.
