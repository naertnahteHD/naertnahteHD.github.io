Judge grants Westminster councilmember Amy Phan-West completion of misdemeanor bribery diversion

Posted Tuesday, 8. September 2026
Recorded Friday, 26. August 2026
West Justice Center, dept. W13 in Westminster, California
For inquiries, email naertnahteHD@gmail.com
Click here for the PDF version of this article


Westminster councilmember Amy Phan-West was charged with one count of misdemeanor bribery in January last year. She was eventually granted a six-month diversion program, requiring her to commit herself to 20 community service hours and a 2-hour ethics class.


The Orange County District Attorney (OCDA) stated that Westminster parking enforcement officers showed up near the area of Dorothy Lane and Melanie Lane to tow an abandoned Jeep that was registered to Phan-West’s husband Jeremy. More than 20 calls were made in 2023 for such vehicles near the area.


Area of Melanie Lane and Dorothy Lane (source: Apple Maps)


OCDA press release except
Published Thursday, 23. January 2025

"After the parking control officers requested a tow truck to tow the Jeep, Phan West is accused of identifying herself and telling the parking control officers now to tow the vehicle and she would move it herself. She is also accused of telling the parking control officers how much she loves the Westminster Police Department, that she is close friends with the Westminster police chief, and that she is in the process of getting raises for police department personnel.

The City Council was engaged in contract negotiations with the Westminster Police Officers’ Association at the time.

Phan West is also accused of showing the parking control officers a Westminster Police Department keychain.

After her statements, the parking officials called to cancel the tow truck and left the abandoned Jeep parked on the street."

Fast forwarding more than a year later, Phan-West appeared with her criminal defense attorney Randy S. Collins before Judge Sherri L. Honer on Friday, 28. August 2026 for the determination of the completion of her diversion.


Deputy District Attorney (Special Prosecutions Unit) Matthew Bradbury presented five exhibits in which Phan-West is accused of ethically and criminally violating the terms of the diversion program. Defense counsel did not have an exhibit to present.


PROSECUTION EXHIBIT nr. 1
After being granted diversion last year, Phan-West made a Facebook post declaring that the misdemeanor bribery case was dropped and that no crime was committed. She was admonished for her use of the word “vindicated” in the post at a later hearing. Bradbury claims that Phan-West was misleading her constituents with the contents of her post. Collins argued that his client was already admonished, and the post consequently had no relevance to the diversion completion.


source: Facebook @amy.west.144181


Judge Honer concluded that the contents of the post were technically not wrong. Agreeing to participate in the diversion program was not an admission of guilt, and completing diversion would mean that her case is dropped. She further comments that Phan-West’s wording was “inartful”.


PROSECUTION EXHIBIT nr. 2
Outside the courtroom, the Westminster City Council and city attorney Scott Porter concluded that Phan-West breached Gov’t Code § 8314. On 25. February 2026, the city council voted 3 yes – 1 no – 1 abstain to ban Phan-West from speaking at city-funded public events. Councilmember Carlos Manzo accused Phan-West of using the publicly-funded 2026 Westminster Tet Parade for political campaigning and distributing campaign literature.

source: Prosecution-filed opposition brief


Bradbury presented this also as a violation of Gov’t Code § 8314 in the courtroom. Prosecution witness Shannon Rackley testified that the defendant and other people were indeed holding pamphlets with Phan-West’s name on it. However, she was not able to determine what the contents of the pamphlets actually were.


Collins determined that the Prosecution was not able to establish (by preponderance of the evidence) that the defendant was handing out campaign material. Judge Honer concluded that there wasn’t anything stopping Phan-West from handing out any kind of pamphlet or material.


Tangentially, Phan-West was eliminated in the CA-45 2026 primary, coming last place with only 4,69 % of the votes.


PROSECUTION EXHIBIT nr. 3
On 10. April 2025, Phan-West published a press release on her Instagram account condemning mayor Charlie Nguyen for cutting her microphone off at a veterans celebration ceremony. While the contents of the press release were not admissible, Bradbury presented the use of the Westminster-branded letterhead to disparage the mayor as a misrepresentation of the official city opinion.


source: Instagram @amyphanwest


Collins argued that the contents of the press release were considered city business, and Judge Honer agreed, citing public affairs.


PROSECUTION EXHIBIT nr. 4
The defendant is further accused of violating the terms of the diversion program by allegedly using her city office for an interview with NBC Los Angeles ahead of the CA-45 2026 primary. Rackley noted that a Westminster district map was on the wall of the interview setting, but she was not able to determine whether the interview actually took place in Phan-West’s city office.


source: nbclosangeles.com


PROSECUTION EXHIBIT nr. 5 (Presented in the YouTube video)
In addition to disparaging the Mayor, Bradbury further alleges that the defendant disparaged the court system at a candidate forum, calling it a “jungle court” for allowing Westminster resident Terry Rains to testify against her. At this forum, Phan-West claims that the sole reason she agreed to diversion was to save OCDA Todd Spitzer’s face. She also describes the parking enforcement officers as “those high school and college students”. Bradbury describes these statements as displaying unethical behavior and misleading voters in the heat of a primary election.


Collins argued that no criminal violations were committed and that the defendant’s statements amounted to nothing more than “throwing dirt”, whilst admitting that she should not have made some of those statements. Judge Honer clarified that the purpose of the hearing was not just to determine whether Phan-West violated any criminal code but also to determine whether Phan-West made any ethical violations.


In the end, Phan-West is found to not have made any false statements. Whilst her words are once again described as awkwardly phrased, they are not misleading or technically wrong. Judge Honer declared that the “jungle court” comment was not an ethical violation and that “people express dissatisfaction with the court all the time”. On a final note, she calls out Bradbury for having an issue with Phan-West’s “alleged bribery” comment and concludes that “that’s exactly what it is, an alleged bribery”.


THE COURT'S OPINION
Judge Honer determined that the Prosecution were not able to meet the burden of proving non-compliance during the diversion program. She ordered the case file be sealed.


Outside the courthouse, Phan-West reiterates she did nothing wrong and thanked God for fighting for her. NOCCCD board trustee Dr Ryan Bent, a supporter of Phan-West during this case, stated that the ruling was a win for the First Amendment.


The OCDA Public Affairs Office did not respond to requests for comment on the Judge's ruling. Westminster resident and Phan-West dissenter Terry Rains declined multiple requests to comment on the Judge's ruling.
