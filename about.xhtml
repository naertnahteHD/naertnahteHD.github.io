About "naertnahte HD"

Since 31. January 2026, "naertnahte HD" is an independent and non-partisan videography proprietorship, covering protests, city council meetings, court hearings, and significant police presence in Orange County.

While the main coverage area currently spans only Huntington Beach and Westminster, some efforts are made to cover all of Orange County.

"naertnahte HD" is expected to be deprecated early May 2027, no later than the end of FY 2027 at the latest. It is no longer financially sustainable to continue the work. (Pending/open cases that were tracked before deprecation will continue to be followed after deprecation until they are closed.)
