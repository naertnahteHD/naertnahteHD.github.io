About "naertnahte HD"

Why are you reading this?

Since 31. January 2026, "naertnahte HD" covers protests, city council meetings, court hearings, and significant police presence in all of Orange County.

While the main coverage area currently spans only Huntington Beach and Westminster, efforts are made to consistently and reliably cover all of Orange County. It can be challenging to follow all the happenings within the 940 sq. mi. of Orange County. As this would suggest, "naertnahte HD" is based in Westminster.

"naertnahte HD" is expected to be deprecated around 21. January 2029, no later than the end of FY 2029 at the latest. This hobby (yes, the professional video camera's sole purpose is for playing with) is not sustainable for one person. The deprecation date intentionally coincides with the 2028 U.S. Presidential Election.

(Pending/open cases that were tracked before deprecation will continue to be followed after deprecation until they are closed.)
