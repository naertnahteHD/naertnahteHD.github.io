Pricing

Are you looking for unuploaded footage or the full raw footage for something you saw on YouTube? Check the availability table to see whether the footage still exists.

If you are a newsroom editor looking for footage, please send an e-mail directly to naertnahteHD@gmail.com for appropriate pricing and licencing.

Understand that the main goal of selling footage is to deter requests for free footage, not to make a profit.

Please note that your information may be made public via the transparency portal (at the sole discretion of "naertnahteHD") if footage is sold to you.

  • You will be notified whether your information will be published before you confirm your purchase.
  • If you are an individual (e.g. a protester or resident), your information will likely be redacted.
  • If you are an activist organisation or newsroom, your information will likely be published.
